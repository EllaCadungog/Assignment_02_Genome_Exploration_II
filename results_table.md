## Results 
The ≥10 kb filtering removed short sequences while retaining 2.344 Gb (95.8%) of the original assembly, reducing contigs from 248 to 213. Contig N50 increased from 34.38 Mb to 42.02 Mb and maximum contig length increased from 81.87 Mb to 111.05 Mb, indicating improved assembly contiguity and reduced fragmentation. Scaffold N50 remained similar (65.78 to 64.49 Mb), while GC content changed minimally (41.49% to 41.18%), suggesting little compositional bias. Overall, the filtered assembly provides a more contiguous and reliable genomic framework for downstream annotation and comparative genomics.
